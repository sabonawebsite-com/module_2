Our story

Slow food, served fast enough for a lunch break.

Habesha Eatery opened on a corner in Bole with one wood table and a jebena that never stopped brewing. Everything on the menu still comes from that first kitchen: berbere ground weekly, injera fermented for three days, and a coffee ceremony we still run in full for anyone who has the time to sit through it.
